Travel Directions

How to Reach Our Oxford Campus

Located in central Oxford at 14 Wellington Square, OX1 2JD, with fast rail and coach links across the UK.

By High-Speed Rail

52 minutes direct from London Paddington or London Marylebone to Oxford Railway Station, followed by a 10-minute walk to Wellington Square.

From Heathrow & Gatwick

The Airline 24-hour luxury express coach departs London Heathrow Terminals 2, 3, and 5 every 20 minutes directly to Gloucester Green (3 mins from campus).

Oxford Park & Ride

Five zero-emission electric Park & Ride terminals surround the Oxford ring road (Pear Tree, Seacourt, Redbridge, Thornhill, Oxford Parkway).
