Entry Standards

Academic & English Proficiency Requirements

We welcome qualifications from UK A-Levels, IB Diploma, US APs, Indian CBSE/ISC, and accredited global bachelor degrees.

Undergraduate Entry

BSc / BA / MEng Honours Criteria

Designed for high-school graduates demonstrating strong analytical foundations in their chosen major subjects.

  • UK A-Levels: AAA to A*A*A depending on faculty
  • IB Diploma: 38–40 points overall with 6,6,6 at Higher Level
  • Indian XII (CBSE/ISC): 90%+ overall in relevant core subjects
  • English Language: IELTS 7.0 overall (no band below 6.5)
Postgraduate & DPhil Entry

MSc / MBA / Doctoral Criteria

Designed for university graduates with strong honours research records or professional leadership experience.

  • UK Honours Degree: First-Class or strong Upper Second (2:1)
  • International GPA: Minimum 3.6/4.0 or 8.0/10.0 CGPA
  • Research Proposal: 1,500-word synopsis for MPhil/DPhil tracks
  • References: Two academic or senior industry endorsements
